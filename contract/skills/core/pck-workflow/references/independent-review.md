# Independent Review

## Assign the review

Define each assignment by its question and the evidence needed. Use one round
by default and honor explicit scope and round counts.

Freeze the file state for the round. Give each reviewer its question, scope,
authorized decisions, baseline, cumulative changes, and repository and contract
locations. Keep the main agent's reasoning and other verdicts out of the briefing.

Obtain the assigned reviews independently and concurrently where supported.
Honor explicit model assignments, choosing the most specific at the same
authority level. Otherwise choose a capable, economical, fast model. If launch
fails, retry with the main agent's model and disclose the retry. A second
failure leaves that review incomplete.

## Inspect and report

Reviewers read the required originals and may inspect history, callers, owners,
and precedents. They work read-only, with no edits, builds, tests, validators,
application runs, commits, or further agents.

Review complete affected units against their applicable clauses and exceptions.
For contract changes, compare the pre-edit contract and authorized decisions.
Limit findings to defects introduced or materially affected by the cumulative
change. Connect each verdict to the governing clause, precise original lines,
and affected behavior, including operation handoffs, declarations, and
expressions where relevant. Report coverage and evidence gaps. A complete
review has the required coverage and evidence.

## Resolve the review

Collect all results from the same file state and assess them before editing.
For a focused recheck, specify the evidence needed and leave the verdict to the
reviewer. Keep further rounds within the agreed limit across all corrections.
At the limit, stop automatic review and report unresolved findings and
incomplete required reviews.

For a veto, report the reviewer, requirement, location, proposal, reason, and
remaining uncertainty. Required reviews and validation stay in force.

Report review scope, rounds, actual models, retries, and unresolved findings.

When recovering context, restore review assignments, models, and round count.
Later rounds may reuse the reviewer's independent context after recovery.
