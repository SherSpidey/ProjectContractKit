# Direct Statements

Describe what an artifact is, what it does, who owns it, how it is used, and
which requirements apply. Make the current structure and behavior explicit.

Keep version comparisons, reconstruction or refactoring narratives, and
past-versus-present explanations out of maintained text. A current requirement
stands on its present meaning and applicable conditions.

When converting material, retain its obligations, scope, conditions,
exceptions, and required observations or execution results. Express that
meaning directly in the maintained contract.

Explain an invariant when it helps someone act correctly. Ownership, lifetime,
numeric assumptions, failure conditions, interface duties, and operational
requirements are useful present facts. Place them beside the requirement or
code they explain.

Apply the same standard to headings, prose, examples, code comments, and
delivery text. State actual validation results and unresolved work accurately.
Remove historical framing while retaining the fact, condition, or decision
the reader needs.
